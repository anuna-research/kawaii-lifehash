# Irregular edge refinement

The owner requested more irregular edge texture. The mask now layers coarse anisotropic turbulence displacement (14 viewBox units) with finer notches (8 units), using distinct digest-derived seeds. This varies contour shape over multiple scales. The same filter applies to the subtle contour and the full additive body/ear alpha union; coat pixels and facial presentation retain their existing behavior. Inline export remains deterministic and self-contained.

The browser suite and 64/256 visual matrix are refreshed for this change. See [[irregular-edge-review]] for independent silhouette, join and clipping findings.

Subsequent owner feedback also requested less odd gaze and removal of the listening circle. Fixed pale eye shells now contain smaller dark pupils; a smooth radial response bounds cursor movement to 1.5 horizontal/1 vertical units. The listening ring markup and animation are removed; attentive expression and gentle nod remain. These refinements share the refreshed tests and visual review.
