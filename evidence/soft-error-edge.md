# Softer error and textured edge refinement

The owner requested a confused rather than angry error expression, textured shape edges, and removal of the flashing grid. Error now has uneven curved raised brows, slightly open eyes, a small uncertain mouth and one gentle head tilt. The preview stage's dot-grid background was removed.

A digest-seeded SVG turbulence displacement textures the additive silhouette union and its subtle contour. The filter only changes silhouette geometry: the complete fingerprint and coat grid retain their original pixels, and ears remain part of the alpha union. Export includes the inline filter, without external assets or runtime randomness. Existing cross-browser image/export checks cover portability; refreshed 64/256 matrices cover edge clipping and readability. See [[soft-error-edge-review]] for independent findings.
